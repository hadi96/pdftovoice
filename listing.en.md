# Play Store listing (en-US)

**App name (max 30):** PDF to Voice

**Short description (max 80):**
Listen to any PDF as an audiobook. Works offline, even with scanned pages.

**Full description (max 4000):**
PDF to Voice turns the PDF books on your phone into audiobooks. Pick a file, press play, and listen while you commute, cook, or rest your eyes. Everything runs on your device, with no account and no internet connection.

Read along as you listen
The text flows on screen like a book, and the sentence being read is highlighted. Tap any sentence to jump there. Your place is saved for every book, so you can always pick up where you left off.

Scanned PDFs work too
Pages without text are recognized on the device with OCR, so photocopied books and scanned documents can be read aloud as well.

The right voice for every book
The language of each book is detected automatically and matched to the voices installed on your phone. Choose a voice, adjust speed and pitch, and the app remembers it per book.

Listen the way you want
• Controls in the notification and on the lock screen, plus headset buttons
• Keeps playing with the screen off
• Sleep timer
• Skip by sentence or by page, or jump to a page number
• Light and dark theme, colors that follow your wallpaper

Private by design
PDF to Voice does not request internet access. Your files, the text inside them, and your reading positions never leave your phone.

Voices are provided by the text-to-speech engine on your device, such as Google Speech Services. Voice quality and the list of languages depend on that engine.
